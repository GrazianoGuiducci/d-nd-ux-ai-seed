# MAIOS Adaptive Semantic Surface Profile

Status: project-specific reference profile for the generic `Adaptive Semantic Surface` candidate.

This file is not the public truth of MAIOS and is not a fixed landing-page template. MAIOS changes rapidly. At implementation time, Codex must inspect the current `maios_it` repository, live routes and current-state documentation before changing structure, copy or claims.

## Working Identity

- **MAIOS** is the public operative ecosystem and coherent point of entry.
- **MMK — MetaMasterKernel** is the kernel that understands, coordinates, verifies and preserves continuity.
- **MAIOS Continuum** or **Semantic Continuity Plane** is the working name for the unifying UI function: one entry point operating across projects, contexts and instances without exposing chat/instance fragmentation at the primary level.

The name is a design label until the operator confirms it for public use.

## Design Synthesis

The profile combines four references, each with a defined responsibility:

- **Mondrian**: proportion, tension, fields and alignment;
- **Bauhaus**: function, hierarchy, directness and geometric discipline;
- **Scandinavian ergonomics**: calm, humane spacing, legibility and low-friction controls;
- **modern Japanese print**: asymmetry, cropping, negative space, limited inks, texture and editorial rhythm.

The result must not look like a collage of styles. It should read as one MAIOS language:

> Structure by system. Space by meaning. Color by intent. Continuity by design.

## Proprietary Visual Signals

### Living Grid

A stable underlying grid with selectively visible lines. Large unboxed areas are required. Cards appear only for manipulable objects or repeated records.

### Semantic Thread

One continuous line crosses copy, diagrams, fields and interaction states. It shows source, relation, transition and resumability. It may change from blue on paper to white on red and green in verified states.

### Perceptual Fields

- paper: understanding and decision support;
- system blue: coordination and depth;
- intent red: declaration and bounded action;
- attention yellow: temporary focus or option;
- adaptive green: verified active state and continuity.

### Semantic Negative Space

Distance and open area express relation, priority and possibility. The primary viewport should never expose every available system plane at once.

## Recommended Surface Modes

### Paper

Editorial, readable and human. Use for manifesto, explanations, decisions and public narrative.

### Ink

Deep inspection and orchestration. Use sparingly for internal mechanics, evidence, state and control.

### Hybrid

The preferred MAIOS signature. Paper carries intent and language; blue/ink carries coordination; red appears at a real decision boundary. Secondary controls enter from edges and disappear without resetting the primary work.

## Landing Architecture

The order below is a behavioral blueprint, not fixed copy.

### 1. Hero: one coherent entry

Show:

- one strong outcome statement;
- current public definition in plain language;
- one primary action;
- one secondary path to MMK or system explanation;
- a simple live diagram or visual field that demonstrates continuity.

Do not show:

- a wall of agent icons;
- every project category;
- a fictitious active orchestration map;
- multiple competing calls to action.

### 2. Current truth and current boundary

Present what is active, what is being integrated and what remains a direction. The design may be expressive, but the state labels must remain factual.

### 3. Semantic continuity demonstration

A diagram should show a real sequence such as:

```text
intent -> context -> MMK coordination -> tools/capabilities -> result -> verification -> resumable state
```

The diagram must have a static accessible summary and reduced-motion state.

### 4. Planes of work

Do not expose eight equal product cards. Group current domains into broader constellations, for example:

- create: projects, research, work and communication;
- govern: finance, time, decisions and verification;
- live and evolve: wellbeing, learning, relations and personal logistics.

Reveal detailed planes through selection, hover/focus depth, local expansion or an inspector.

### 5. MMK field

Use a system-blue field with paper/white text. Explain the kernel through a concise sequence:

- understands;
- coordinates;
- verifies;
- learns;
- preserves continuity.

Continuity is the condition across the sequence, not merely the last feature.

### 6. UI evidence

Show a credible interface state rather than a decorative dashboard. The primary area carries the current intent. Context and current project remain visible. Verification and management details stay in a recoverable side panel or bottom sheet.

### 7. Evolutionary design manifesto

Use an editorial block with high negative space. State how the interface adapts while user control, accessibility and action boundaries remain stable.

### 8. Closing field

Use one declaration, one next action and the semantic thread continuing beyond the viewport. Avoid a dense marketing footer.

## Motion Storyboard

### Hero entry

- the semantic thread draws from the current intent toward the MMK field;
- labels appear only after their relation becomes clear;
- the main call to action is immediately available and never waits for animation.

### Scroll transition

- paper and blue fields exchange prominence through a short cross-field transition;
- the previous section leaves a visible alignment or thread trace;
- no scroll-jacking or parallax is required.

### Plane selection

- selecting a constellation brings related planes closer;
- unrelated details recede without disappearing from the navigation model;
- the change is reversible and keyboard accessible.

### Control reveal

- an edge rail opens into an inspector;
- the primary surface remains visible where space permits;
- focus enters the inspector and returns to the trigger on close.

### Decision boundary

- red appears only when a choice, confirmation or explicit action becomes current;
- the field contains the consequence, alternatives and safe exit;
- confirmation is not encoded by color alone.

## Diagram Families

Recommended:

- continuity thread;
- source-to-result flow;
- intent lineage;
- semantic planes stack;
- context constellation;
- verification timeline;
- fragmentation versus continuity comparison.

Avoid:

- generic glowing neural networks;
- orbit diagrams without real relations;
- decorative agent swarms;
- animated activity that is not derived from current product state.

## Typography

Use a large, direct display face for short manifesto lines and a calmer open sans-serif for explanations and controls. A monospaced face may identify evidence, state, versions or machine-readable data.

Do not use Japanese text as decoration. When Japanese appears, it must be accurate, translated and conceptually relevant.

## Texture And Illustration

Use limited print texture, halftone, offset registration and monoline illustration. Texture should disappear around small text and controls. Illustration may reference Japanese editorial print without relying on repeated Fuji, rising-sun, wave and seal motifs in the same viewport.

Campaign images may be more expressive than the core product UI.

## Codex Adoption Sequence

1. Read `docs/ADAPTIVE_SEMANTIC_SURFACE.md` in this seed repository.
2. Inspect current `maios_it` content and browser behavior.
3. Inventory current sections as content/state contracts, not visual boxes.
4. Map them to the landing architecture above without rewriting claims by default.
5. Add the token file and theme roles.
6. Implement the living grid and semantic thread before adding animation.
7. Add one real diagram with accessible summary.
8. Add progressive disclosure for secondary specifications and management state.
9. Verify Paper, Ink and Hybrid modes.
10. Run responsive, keyboard, reduced-motion and contrast checks.
11. Record the change and rollback path in the target project's current-state documentation.

## Acceptance Signal

The profile is working when the page feels less like a dark technical report or a static Japanese poster and more like a coherent, living system in which:

- the user sees one entry point;
- current truth remains legible;
- system depth is available but not imposed;
- the visual rhythm explains relationships;
- the interface can evolve without losing identity.
