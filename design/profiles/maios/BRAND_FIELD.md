# MAIOS brand field — design use

Profile version: 0.1.0 · observed source: 23 September 2026.
State: current-source interpretation for controlled design work; new layouts and
copy remain candidates, not a replacement for the production site.

## Identity before style

MAIOS should become recognisable through a coherent relationship between the
work, its continuation and the person's available action. The operator selects
showing the object rather than seeking consensus or constructing defensive
marketing. Source, capability and effect remain truthful without turning the
first public view into an internal checklist.

The launch receiver often knows ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, sometimes a coding
host, and need not know semantic kernels. Use recognisable work and actions
before internal vocabulary. This does not authorise an invented claim that those
products have no memory, cannot continue work or all behave identically.

MPK and kernel_chat have distinct entry conditions and can participate in one
work setting. Do not draw them as automatic synchronisation between providers.
A persistent source, access and receiver capabilities remain part of the actual
relation. Brand recognition must not imply endorsement by another provider.

## Reusable expressive relation

Use unequal fields, deliberate interval, readable type, a clear subject and a
small number of meaningful accents. Preserve room for an encounter rather than
fill every region with cards. An expressive or animated artifact can remain
faithful; there is no requirement for a deliberately unremarkable appearance.

Historical studies reach Mondrian for field balance, Bauhaus for functional
reduction, Japanese print for interval/cropping, Scandinavian ergonomics for
recovery and ease, Kandinsky for relational tension and colour adjacency for
perceptual differentiation. They remain operators, not literal style imitation.

The old adaptive-surface PR proposed ivory/indigo roles and Paper/Ink/Hybrid
modes. Later source knowledge distinguished a relational chromatic field from
fixed theme identities. Neither the old palette nor a calibration laboratory
becomes the new launch page. Preserve their useful method, not their entire form.

## Surface differences

A social artifact carries an intelligible relation before a click; it need not
teach the theory. A linked product entry can give depth and a next action. An
email remains understandable without an image. A technical or research artifact
can legitimately carry more precise language. The same identity does not mean
the same composition or copy on each surface.

Do not add versions to a durable master unless they alter use. A launch date can
be described when scheduled or observed; "nobody knows it yet" and "no reviews
exist" are not inferable from the fact that an announcement is new.

## Profile learning

Actual operator decisions and qualified receiver observations update this
profile. Generic medium/interaction rules return to their design faculties.
Keep current accepted form, candidate explorations and dated evidence distinct.
The first prototype does not constitute brand approval or a user study.
