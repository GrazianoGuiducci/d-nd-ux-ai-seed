# MAIOS Launch Visual System — candidate 0.1

Date: 2026-09-23
Owner: D-ND Design Kernel + MAIOS brand profile
State: launch candidate, not published

## Receiver field

Primary first encounter: people who already use conversational AI and, in some
cases, AI coding/project hosts. Prior knowledge of semantic kernels is not
required.

The first frame starts from work they recognize, not from kernel taxonomy.

## Source relations carried

- useful work can leave durable source/context/reason/method relations;
- a reusable difference from real work can become competence knowledge;
- `kernel_chat` is the natural entry when a receiving AI has a persistent/custom
  operating entry plus a durable source it can reach;
- MAIOS Project Kernel is the natural entry when the continuing surface is the
  project/repository/filesystem;
- the two products can be used separately; joint use does not imply automatic
  synchronization between providers or applications;
- exact product state, installation and proof remain with their repositories.

## Visual grammar

Current MAIOS source profile supplies air cyan, green substrate, warm ground,
cream reading surfaces, dark blue-green ink, violet focus and restrained warm
accents. The launch family uses unequal fields, large quiet areas, short display
statements and one clear path through each artifact.

Generated imagery is not required for the first family. Exact labels, product
names and links remain editable in HTML/SVG. Expressive image-generation can be
added later when it carries a relation that the deterministic layer does not.

## Artifact family

1. Landing first encounter — one full first viewport, then progressive depth.
2. Product cards — MPK, kernel_chat, combined relation.
3. Social visual 01 — continuity beyond one session.
4. Social visual 02 — chat and project as two distinct entries.
5. Social visual 03 — experience becoming reusable capability.

## Surface jobs

Social: one recognizable relation in a glance; the post copy supplies the
specific source/context and link.

Email/creator outreach: text-first message remains sufficient if images are
blocked; the landing carries the visual encounter.

Landing: recognition -> choose natural entry -> understand what persists ->
reach source/product.

Product card: compact orientation, not full product documentation.

## Boundaries

No public date, review count, traction claim or “nobody knows it yet” statement
is embedded. No visual connects named providers as if they synchronize. No
production deployment or public URL is implied by these files.
