
The receiving field assumes familiarity with conversational AI and sometimes AI
coding/project hosts, not prior understanding of semantic kernels. The first
encounter therefore begins from continuity of real work, not kernel taxonomy.

The current medium choice is HTML/CSS/SVG with editable text. Three.js/WebGL is
not rejected; it was not selected because the present first-encounter relation
does not lose material meaning in 2D. Image generation remains available when a
future composition needs an expressive field that deterministic geometry cannot
carry as effectively.

## Readback that changed the design

The initial two-entry social draft drew one continuous line between
`kernel_chat` and Project Kernel while a caption denied automatic
synchronisation. The visual grammar introduced the very relation the copy was
trying to exclude.

The corrected artifact removes the app-to-app bridge and presents two distinct
entries. Their possible participation in one work setting is stated in text,
conditional on the setup exposing both relations.

This reusable correction belongs to spatial/perceptual design:

```text
shared work relation
!= direct provider-to-provider connection

a visual edge
-> carries a predicate
-> must not be used merely to imply conceptual relatedness
```

## Verification scope

The final deterministic sources passed 48/48 local Chromium checks covering
responsive overflow, entry-state consistency, keyboard/focus, reduced motion,
product-card reflow, SVG rendering, page errors and absence of remote asset
requests. Exact source hashes are in `VERIFICATION.json`.

first-encounter comprehension, product runtime behaviour or publication.
