# Source-Grounded Storyboard

Version: 0.1.0 · Semantic owner: D-ND Design Kernel / this method.

## Find the event that can be seen

Recover the subject, receiving situation and the relationship whose change
matters. Find what becomes different over time: an interruption and return,
a correction participating in later work, a decision opening a new view, a
source becoming relevant, an action and its consequence. Do not begin with an
inventory of chapters or an obligatory brand intro.

A temporal form is useful when seeing the change teaches something the static
composition does not. If an infographic or direct interaction is sufficient,
keep that simpler form. A promotional request does not itself require video.

The source owner supplies actual events/capabilities and their limits. The
writing owner co-forms narration, captions and language. Perceptual Composition
supplies art direction. This method owns the event, scene relation, temporal
hierarchy and generation brief, not voice or product facts.

## Preserve temporal truth

Distinguish an observed event, an illustrative reconstruction and a possible
scenario. Show the appropriate status where it affects interpretation. Do not
animate activity, autonomous work, cross-host transfer or success that has not
occurred or whose mechanism is absent. A labelled schematic may explain a
possibility without claiming a captured product demonstration.

Keep what happened, what was understood then and what is understood now separate
when a later consequence changes the interpretation. Do not edit a failure out
of a demonstration merely to make the intended theory appear inevitable.

## Compose time and attention

Each scene must perform a perceptual function: locate the subject, expose a
relationship, show a change, keep orientation, make a consequence available or
support a next action. There is no universal duration or fixed sequence.

Maintain visible origin and destination when motion changes the reading plane.
Give the receiver enough time to recognise the relevant state before it changes.
Direct manipulation remains immediate; explanatory transitions may be slower.
Avoid competing reveals that leave attention and keyboard focus on different
objects. A selected state should not retain the prior state's content during a
transition.

For a montage, narration and visual action should complement rather than repeat
one another. For a walkthrough, the actual control and its result must be visible.
For a conceptual animation, geometry must preserve the source relation. Choose
cadence from the event and surface rather than stretching an arbitrary runtime.

## Execution and alternatives

Provide a compact scene relation only as detailed as the selected executor
needs: event, visual action, relevant wording/caption slots, transition and
source/reality boundary. A short loop may need a single scene description; a
technical demonstration may require exact steps and captured evidence.

Use the actual image, animation, video or web tools available. Do not claim that
an LLM has rendered a video, recorded a product or run an external generator
merely because the storyboard is complete. External generated material is a
candidate whose added meanings are reviewed against the source.

Keep important words editable and versions beside the technical entry unless
they materially affect the event. Supply captions/transcript for audiovisual
meaning and a static/reduced-motion equivalent for interactive motion. The
alternative preserves the informational relationship, not necessarily the same
sequence of pictures.

## Learn from temporal consequences

Inspect where the subject becomes clear, which change is actually seen, where
pacing obstructs understanding, which transition invents causality and whether
the next action remains natural. A pleasant animation is not evidence that a
receiver understood the system.

Return reusable timing/scene knowledge here, interaction/focus defects to
Interaction Quality, wording to the writing owner, source corrections to the
represented object and platform performance to its distribution owner.

This source generalises the earlier launch-native storyboard faculty. It does
not silently retire that source, absorb a YouTube channel operator, or establish
that every future launch needs an audiovisual package.
