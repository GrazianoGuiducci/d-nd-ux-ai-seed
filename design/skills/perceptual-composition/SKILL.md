# Perceptual Composition

Version: 0.4.0 · Semantic owner: D-ND Design Kernel / this method.

## Begin with what must become visible

Acquire the actual object and the relevant operator intention before proposing
geometry. Establish what the receiver recognises now, the relation the artifact
should make available, and the useful next action or understanding. Do not ask a
novice to design the solution. Use known context; recover only a missing fact
that would change the design.

Familiarity is an entry coordinate, not a verdict on what may exist. A person
may recognise ChatGPT without knowing a semantic kernel. Begin with the work
or situation they can recognise, then let the new relation appear. Do not force
an abstract definition, or invent an opponent to make the object defensible.

A useful internal sentence is: "This expression lets this receiver perceive X
through Y." It is not mandatory copy. If X is only a product label or Y is only
"a beautiful animation", deepen the source or revise the visual question.

## Awareness-bearing visual contraction

Visual syntax is downstream of understanding. Let the source, product truth,
operator meaning and relevant consequences deepen the design object before
reducing it to a social card, hero, diagram or campaign convention.

```text
deeper source-grounded understanding
-> determine what relation must become perceptible
-> preserve subject + dominant dynamic + consequence
-> contract into the simplest visual syntax that keeps those relations true
-> keep unused depth reachable rather than forcing it into the frame
```

The design may therefore become simpler as awareness grows. Simplicity does not
mean generic minimalism, fewer words by default or removal of conceptual depth.
It means that the composition knows which relation carries the event and can
remove what no longer helps the receiver perceive it.

Advertising heuristics such as vivid colour, high-quality imagery, explicit
CTA, short copy and mobile optimisation are **surface-relative possibilities**.
Use them when the selected encounter benefits from them; never treat them as the
ontology of a social artifact. Attention can be created through tension,
silence, scale, interval, sequence, image, typography, colour, movement or
another relation consistent with the object and brand field.

Mobile legibility remains a real receiving condition. Preserve semantic parity
at feed scale, but do not let the mobile constraint flatten the underlying
relation. A visual can be compact while the competence that formed it retains
much greater source depth.

For a first encounter, the receiver should be able to recover what the artifact
is about before the caption repairs it. The caption can deepen, contextualise or
open the next relation; it should not supply the missing subject of an otherwise
ambiguous visual.


## Social depth image families

For the MAIOS social field, preserve three image functions without turning them
into fixed templates:

```text
announcement / signal image
  -> subject + one need / tension / transformation
  -> immediate recognition;

pill image
  -> one semantic relation only
  -> retention of a capability / distinction;

consequential synthesis
  -> several relations whose combined consequence is the object
  -> route to Source-Grounded Infographic or temporal faculty when needed.
```

The first two are not merely simpler versions of an infographic. Their
perceptual job is different. An announcement can use image, typography, spatial
tension or another carrier to establish one recognizable event. A pill should
make one relation memorable without collapsing it into a generic slogan.

A synthesis may be denser because its job is reasoning. Density is permitted
when every visible object participates in the consequential relation and the
receiver can recover the reading path.

Target adaptation changes the receiving situation, not the MAIOS brand or
product truth:

```text
same source relation
+ selected domain / target competence
-> different recognizable entry / evidence / emphasis
-> selected image family
```

Do not preassign colours, symbols or a MAIOS feature to each target.


## Present, direction and horizon in one visual field

When a public artifact carries both current product truth and a wider MAIOS
direction, do not flatten the horizon merely because it is unfamiliar and do
not render it as if it were already present.

Use perceptual hierarchy to distinguish:

```text
present capability
-> selected development direction
-> open longer-term horizon
```

The horizon may be visually strong when positioning depends on it. Its strength
does not change its epistemic status. Distance, layering, rhythm, labels,
progression or another visual relation can communicate possibility without
turning it into a product feature or forecast.


## Select a form, not a fashionable tool

Distinguish the artifact's job from its carrier. A short message can lead to a
rich page. A social post needs value before the click. A product card supports
recognition and selection, not a full architecture tutorial. A technical diagram
may be the primary artifact rather than an accessory. No image can be the right
answer when the text already performs the job or the surface will not show it.

Select motion when temporal change carries meaning, spatial representation when
simultaneous relations matter, interaction when the person's exploration changes
what can be understood. Use 3D when depth, viewpoint or manipulation contributes
something a simpler projection loses. A tool mentioned by the operator remains
an available possibility, not an obligation to deploy it everywhere.

For an email designed to survive disabled images, make the message and its
primary link sufficient. This is a chosen failure-tolerant design condition,
not a claim that every email client blocks images. Media can live at the linked
destination; do not make an attachment necessary for initial comprehension.

## Compose hierarchy and visual syntax

Choose a dominant object and an intentional relationship between its surrounding
fields. Use type, scale, space, alignment, value and colour together. Unequal
areas can express different information weights; equal cards imply equivalence
and should not be the default for unrelated roles.

Typography must survive the actual viewing scale. Test the final crop, wrapping,
text length and language instead of assuming that a desktop artboard will read
in a feed. Distinguish display text, reading text and controls without turning
small explanatory language into illegible decorative metadata.

Negative space can locate focus, separation and an open possibility. It need not
be filled. Nor should it carry an essential hidden instruction by absence alone.
A shape can carry a relation, a rhythm or atmosphere; decorative choices are
allowed, but must not masquerade as state, evidence or architecture.

Keep perceptual channels distinguishable when they encode meaning:

- hue or other markers can identify a role;
- luminance can distinguish reading planes;
- contrast and scale can locate attention;
- proximity, edges and intervals can express relations and boundaries;
- motion can express a change over time.

These are available encodings, not fixed colour semantics. Do not make colour
alone carry role, urgency, certainty and action simultaneously. Do not imply
model confidence through opacity unless that confidence is actually supplied
and defined by its owner.

## Brand is a profile, not universal method

For a relational chromatic field, chromatic-vector discipline and view-state
behaviour, read
[the Relational Chromatic Design reference](../../references/relational-chromatic-design.md).

Read the selected project's present profile and inspect the relevant existing
surface. Preserve what has actually been approved, distinguish exploratory
studies and later observed implementation, and choose the smallest coherent
extension for this artifact. A historical preference does not silently replace
the current public identity.

Artistic references can supply operations: field balance, functional reduction,
interval, adjacency, tension, cropping or temporal rhythm. They are not a
requirement to imitate a painting or to apply the same style to every domain.
A vivid identity is compatible with legibility. "Not hype" is not a rule to make
every expression quiet, colourless or institutionally generic.

If no project brand exists, form a labelled candidate from the actual subject.
Do not inherit MAIOS colours merely because the design method originates here.

## Co-form with writing and representation

Bring the writing owner into the forming artifact, not only a final polish.
Keep source phrases where they say the thing directly. Let a layout request
shorter text without changing the claim; let a necessary distinction change the
layout rather than shrinking type until it fits.

Use the spatial or temporal faculty when a relation needs that depth. Do not
turn their presence into an obligatory infographic/video pair. If the first
visual introduces a false relation, reopen that composition while preserving
valid copy, current product identity and the chosen purpose.

## Give the executor enough to work

A formed brief carries the perceptual thesis, selected composition, exact or
editable text slots, subject/asset provenance, state/reality boundary and the
constraints that can change rendering. Put technical detail in an implementation
layer only when that executor needs it.

Generated imagery can supply a visual field or illustration. Exact product
names, controls, versions and destinations remain editable when errors would
matter. Do not bake volatile facts into a reusable master. The generator's
attractive new connection is a proposal for review, not a discovered mechanism.

A static equivalent must retain the relevant meaning when motion, WebGL,
external assets or scripting are unavailable. This need not be a pixel-identical
screenshot: a labelled linear relationship may be the better equivalent.

## Observe and learn

Read the artifact at its real size and context. Record what actually changed:
an unreadable label, a misleading connection, lost action, excessive competing
focus, a useful recognisable motif, or a relation the first brief missed. Do not
substitute approval or engagement counts for comprehension.

A correction to visual reasoning returns here; a palette or mark belongs to the
project profile; a copy insight to the writing owner; an interaction defect to
Interaction Quality. If this method already carried the necessary knowledge,
repair the route that failed to make it participate. New evidence can reverse an
earlier design without rewriting the earlier observation.

First controlled learning: an illustrative bridge between two named apps can
look like automatic synchronisation. Show the durable work/source relation and
its real access conditions instead of decorating an unverified app-to-app link.
