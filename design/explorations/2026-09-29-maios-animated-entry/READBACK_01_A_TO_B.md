# Readback 01 — Prototype A -> Prototype B

Date: 2026-09-29

## Operator-observed Prototype A

The operator's real browser readback shows:

- the motion is not perceptually evident;
- individual elements are still rough;
- the composition needs adjustment.

The screenshots also expose deeper relations that should be corrected before
polishing components.

## What the first resultant reveals

### 1. Parallax was too small to establish a motion language

Prototype A moved visual planes only by a few pixels. Even where the mechanics
were active, the effect could read as static at ordinary desktop scale.

This is not merely a request for "more animation". A motion that is not
perceptible cannot carry orientation or meaning.

### 2. The title consumes the visual field

At ordinary wide desktop height, the headline occupies most of the first
viewport. The lower copy/action is pushed into the mountain region and loses
contrast and breathing room.

The intended relation was text + horizon. The rendered relation became
headline over landscape.

### 3. The landscape is still generic

The ridge stack gives depth, but it does not yet explain why this is MAIOS
rather than a technology, climate or travel visual.

The next test must make the landscape change function: abstract complexity
should become named field relations and then usable entry points.

### 4. The first section and product cards are different design grammars

The hero is atmospheric and spatial; the next section becomes a conventional
three-card product grid. This breaks the identity at exactly the point where
the concept should become operational.

### 5. The full dark final section behaves like another theme

It is visually strong but currently reads as a second brand state rather than
a local depth vector of the same chromatic field.

### 6. The prototype used an approximate mark

The first prototype redrew the MAIOS mark. Prototype B instead embeds the exact
current `maios_it/logo_40px.png` source asset. Brand assets should not be
approximated when the owner asset is reachable.

## Prototype B hypothesis

```text
horizon as first state
-> visible scroll-controlled separation of layers
-> named field relations emerge from those layers
-> the warm attractor becomes MAIOS focus
-> three real entry routes emerge from the same scene
-> next section keeps one field and lets the visitor change focus
```

Prototype B deliberately removes the full dark closing theme and the equal
three-card deck. It is a motion/identity test, not a finished landing.

## Candidate reusable learning

Do not promote yet:

> Motion selected to carry orientation must create an observable semantic or
> perceptual delta at the real viewing scale. "Technically animated" is not
> evidence that motion participates.

This belongs to Cognitive Motion if later non-identical use confirms it.
