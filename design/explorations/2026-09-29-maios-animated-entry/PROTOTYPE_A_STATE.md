## Motion boundary

- pointer parallax is bounded and affects only the visual field;
- scroll changes the depth relation and horizon underline;
- one requestAnimationFrame writer updates the CSS variables;
- IntersectionObserver stops pointer-driven work when the hero is out of view;
- reduced-motion removes parallax and transition dependence;
- the semantic state and actions remain available without motion.
